## cover

Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: original wide cover illustration for a Korean historical political management videogame set in 1960-1979, not a remake of any existing cover. Create a cinematic painterly editorial illustration, landscape 16:9. Foreground a wooden government desk with paper dossiers and a fountain pen, only a mature Korean statesman's back and shoulder seen at the far right, civilian dark suit, face not shown. Through a wide window, an imagined early-1960s Seoul city of tiled roofs and modest brick buildings, a distant industrial riverfront. Warm ivory paper, deep petrol teal, aged brass, restrained vermilion, subtle print grain. Balanced thoughtful mood: civic responsibility, rebuilding, uncertainty. Left half mostly uncluttered dark teal shadow for title overlay by code. No text, no lettering, no logos, no flags, no modern skyscrapers, no weapons, no heroic salutes. This is openly fictional illustration, not an archival photograph.
